# Atlas — Pricing Plans

## Free Plan

The free plan supports one knowledge base, up to ten uploaded documents, and
one thousand questions per month. Vectorized search is limited to a single
embedding model.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan supports unlimited knowledge bases and documents. It includes
access to all embedding models, advanced reranking, and priority queuing for
generation requests. It is designed for individual researchers and small teams.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan adds on-premise deployment, role-based access control,
single sign-on, and a support SLA. Documents are indexed and served within the
customer's own infrastructure, and no document content leaves the deployment.

## Comparing plans

The main differences are document limits, the availability of on-premise
deployment, advanced retrieval features, and administrative controls. The Pro
and Enterprise plans both support multiple knowledge bases. On-premise
deployment is exclusive to Enterprise.
